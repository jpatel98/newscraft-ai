# DVE VO (voice-over only, starts on pictures)

A breakout voice-over that begins directly on visuals. There is no ON CAM. Lead with the strongest visual or development, keep it especially tight, include impact or a next step where relevant. Usually 15 to 30 seconds. Then Super (when there is a location) and Banner.

## Format
{VO}
A PEDESTRIAN IS DEAD AFTER A COLLISION ON HIGHWAY 404 IN NORTH YORK.
O-P-P SAY IT HAPPENED AROUND 6 A-M IN THE SOUTHBOUND LANES NEAR FINCH AVENUE.
INVESTIGATORS BELIEVE OTHER VEHICLES MAY HAVE ALSO HIT THE MAN... AND DID NOT REMAIN AT THE SCENE.
POLICE ARE ASKING DRIVERS WHO PASSED THROUGH THE AREA AROUND THAT TIME TO CHECK THEIR VEHICLES FOR DAMAGE.

Super:
North York

Banner:
Pedestrian killed on Highway 404 in North York

## Examples of the house style
{VO}
IT'S DAY SIX OF AN ONGOING FIRE AT TWO HIGHRISE TOWERS IN TORONTO'S THORNCLIFFE PARK NEIGHBOURHOOD.
FIRE OFFICIALS SAY NO PROGRESS HAS BEEN MADE IN EXTINGUISHING THE FLAMES, WHICH ARE SMOULDERING IN THE INSULATION BETWEEN THE WALLS.
HUNDREDS OF RESIDENTS REMAIN DISPLACED WITH NO TIMELINE TO RETURN.
THE CITY HAS EXTENDED EMERGENCY HOTEL SUPPORT UNTIL THURSDAY.

{VO}
TWO STOPS ON TORONTO'S SUBWAY MAP WILL HAVE NEW NAMES STARTING THIS SUNDAY.
DUNDAS STATION WILL FORMALLY BECOME T-M-U STATION, WHILE EGLINTON WEST WILL BE RENAMED CEDARVALE STATION.
THE T-M-U CHANGE IS PART OF A PARTNERSHIP WITH THE UNIVERSITY, WHILE THE CEDARVALE NAME REFLECTS ITS CONNECTION TO THE FUTURE EGLINTON CROSSTOWN.

{VO}
A NEW REPORT IS PAINTING A GRIM PICTURE OF ONTARIO'S MANUFACTURING SECTOR... SAYING THE SHARE OF JOBS HAS DROPPED TO LEVELS NOT SEEN SINCE 1976.
THE REPORT FINDS THE SECTOR HAS LOST MORE THAN 20-THOUSAND JOBS IN SEVEN OF THE LAST EIGHT QUARTERS.
OFFICIALS BLAME FACTORS LIKE U-S TARIFFS AND AUTO PLANT RETOOLING, THOUGH THE FORD GOVERNMENT INSISTS IT IS STILL ATTRACTING BILLIONS IN NEW INVESTMENTS.
