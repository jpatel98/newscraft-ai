# OCVO (on camera, then voice-over)

The anchor starts on camera, then reads over pictures. Usually 25 to 35 seconds unless the producer asks otherwise: three to five short VO sentences.

## Structure
- {ON CAM}: one strong sentence (two short ones only if a second fact is essential). It gives the newest or most important development and tells viewers why the story matters now. Do not cram every fact into it. Lead directly with the event.
- {VO}: adds detail rather than repeating the ON CAM. Establish where and when, explain the main development, include impact, finish with the investigation, a next step or useful context. Choose only what viewers need.
- Then Super (when there is a location) and Banner.

## Format
{ON CAM}
A MAN HAS DIED AFTER A COLLISION INVOLVING AN E-SCOOTER AND A VEHICLE IN TORONTO'S PORT LANDS.

{VO}
IT HAPPENED AROUND 7:40 P-M NEAR LESLIE STREET AND UNWIN AVENUE.
PARAMEDICS RUSHED THE MAN TO A TRAUMA CENTRE IN CRITICAL CONDITION.
POLICE SAY HE LATER DIED IN HOSPITAL.
THE DRIVER REMAINED AT THE SCENE.

Super:
Toronto

Banner:
Man dies after e-scooter crash in Port Lands

## Examples of the house style
{ON CAM}
A SPRING ICE STORM IS HITTING SOUTHERN ONTARIO TONIGHT... CAUSING WIDESPREAD POWER OUTAGES.
{VO}
ENVIRONMENT CANADA ISSUED FREEZING RAIN WARNINGS FOR PARTS OF THE G-T-A... INCLUDING VAUGHAN, MARKHAM AND BRAMPTON.
MORE THAN 35-THOUSAND HOMES HAVE ALREADY LOST POWER.
AUTHORITIES ARE URGING RESIDENTS TO AVOID UNNECESSARY TRAVEL UNTIL CONDITIONS IMPROVE MONDAY MORNING.

{ON CAM}
A BIG CHANGE IS ON THE WAY FOR POLICING IN YORK REGION.
{VO}
BEGINNING NEXT YEAR, THE SERVICE WILL ROLL OUT BODY-WORN CAMERAS FOR FRONT-LINE OFFICERS.
THE SERVICE SAYS CAMERAS WILL INCREASE TRANSPARENCY... AND HELP BUILD TRUST BETWEEN THE ORGANIZATION AND COMMUNITY MEMBERS.
BODY-WORN CAMERAS ARE ALREADY BEING USED BY OFFICERS IN TORONTO, PEEL AND DURHAM, AMONG OTHERS.

{ON CAM}
HAMILTON POLICE ARE SEARCHING FOR TWO SUSPECTS TONIGHT... IN A HATE-MOTIVATED ASSAULT INVESTIGATION.
{VO}
THE PAIR WERE CAPTURED ON CAMERA ON SATURDAY, OCTOBER 25TH.
ACCORDING TO POLICE, THAT'S WHEN A TRANSGENDER PERSON WAS ASSAULTED ON HESS STREET SOUTH.
ANYONE WITH INFORMATION IS URGED TO CONTACT HAMILTON POLICE OR CRIMESTOPPERS.

{ON CAM}
ONE OF THE PLAYERS ACQUITTED IN THE WORLD JUNIOR HOCKEY SEX ASSAULT TRIAL IS EXPECTED TO MAKE HIS RETURN TO THE N-H-L.
{VO}
THE VEGAS GOLDEN KNIGHTS HAVE RECALLED GOALTENDER CARTER HART FROM THE AMERICAN HOCKEY LEAGUE... AHEAD OF TOMORROW'S GAME AGAINST CHICAGO.
THE 27-YEAR-OLD IS ELIGIBLE TO PLAY AFTER BEING REINSTATED BY THE N-H-L.
HE WAS ACQUITTED OF SEXUAL ASSAULT LAST SUMMER... ALONG WITH DILLON DUBE, MICHAEL MCLEOD, CAL FOOTE AND ALEX FORMENTON.

Bad ON CAM (overloaded): WITH JUST ONE WEEK TO GO BEFORE POSSIBLE NEW U-S TARIFFS... CANADA AND THE U-S ARE NOW CONTINUING NEGOTIATIONS AS PRESSURE BUILDS TO REACH A TRADE DEAL.
Good ON CAM: TRADE TALKS CONTINUE IN WASHINGTON... AS PRESSURE BUILDS ON BOTH SIDES OF THE BORDER TO REACH AN AGREEMENT.
