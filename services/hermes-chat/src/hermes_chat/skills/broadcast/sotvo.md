# Scripts with a clip: OCVO SOTVO, VO SOTVO, SUVO SOTVO, OC/SOT/VO

Use the structure the producer names. Do not add a banner, a second SOT or extra sections unless asked.

## Structures
OCVO SOTVO:
{ON CAM}
[ONE CONCISE ANCHOR LEAD.]
{VO}
[MAIN FACTS AND CONTEXT.]
[NATURAL SETUP INTO THE SOT.]
{SOT}
[NAME / TITLE or clip note with timecodes]
"QUOTE."
{VO}
[ONE OR TWO LINES ADVANCING OR CLOSING THE STORY.]

VO SOTVO: the same without the {ON CAM}.
SUVO SOTVO: {SU} with a sound note ([NAT SOUND / VISUAL], about 3 to 5 seconds of useful sound), then {VO}, {SOT}, {VO}.
OC/SOT/VO: {ON CAM}, {SOT}, {VO}.

## The SOT
- It must advance the story: emotion, explanation, accountability, reaction, firsthand experience, a clear position. Never a clip that repeats what the anchor just said.
- Usually 5 to 12 seconds. Preserve supplied timecodes. When choosing from a transcript, give the IN and OUT timecodes when possible and prefer emotion, a concise explanation, the central call to action, accountability, memorable natural language. If a speaker becomes emotional, let the moment breathe.
- Never alter the meaning of a quote. Never manufacture a quote. If no clip or transcript is supplied, write a clip placeholder such as [MICHAEL LINDSAY CLIP] instead of inventing words.

## Writing into and out of the SOT
- The line right before the SOT tees up why viewers are about to hear this person (WORKERS SAY THEY ARE STRUGGLING TO KEEP UP.). Never write HERE'S WHAT THEY HAD TO SAY.
- Do not state the SOT's main point right before or right after it.
- The closing VO moves the story forward in one or two sentences: next steps, investigation status, the other side's response, an upcoming vote or date, an unresolved question. Do not summarize the story again or force a clever ending.

## Example
{ON CAM}
COMMUNITY MEMBERS IN PICKERING CAME TOGETHER THIS AFTERNOON... DEMANDING ACTION AGAINST ANTI-MUSLIM HATE.
{VO}
THE RALLY STARTED AT 2:30 P-M OUTSIDE THE POLICE STATION ON KINGSTON ROAD... ORGANIZED BY LOCAL ADVOCATES FOLLOWING A RECENT ATTACK ON A MUSLIM WOMAN IN AJAX.
{SOT}
[00:00:10 RALLY ORGANIZER]
"WE'RE HERE TO STAND TOGETHER AND SHOW THAT THIS KIND OF HATE HAS NO PLACE IN OUR COMMUNITY."
{VO}
POLICE ARE STILL INVESTIGATING LAST WEEK'S ATTACK... INCLUDING WHETHER IT SHOULD BE LABELLED A HATE CRIME.

{ON CAM}
FIRST THERE WAS A FIGHT OVER SPEED CAMERAS.
NOW, SOME MUNICIPALITIES ARE BATTLING THE PROVINCE OVER THE SIZE OF ROAD SAFETY SIGNS.
{VO}
THE LARGE WARNING SIGNS GIVEN TO TORONTO AND OTHER CITIES WERE MEANT TO REPLACE SPEED CAMERAS AFTER THEY WERE BANNED BY QUEEN'S PARK.
BUT THOSE MUNICIPALITIES SAY THEY WOULD RATHER KEEP THE CAMERAS... BECAUSE THEY SAY THE CAMERAS PROVIDE BETTER PROTECTION FOR PEDESTRIANS.
{SOT}
[00:00:53 -- 00:01:06 | BIG SIGN COUNCIL CLIP]
{VO}
THE PROVINCE IS ALSO PROVIDING 210-MILLION DOLLARS FOR INFRASTRUCTURE CHANGES... INCLUDING SPEED BUMPS... FOR MUNICIPALITIES THAT WANT THEM.
