# Intros, live throws and top-of-show

Package intros, live reporter throws, OCVO intros and live throws, OC-SOT-VO into a reporter, dual-anchor intros, top-of-show opens, kickers, late-news rewrites, national and affiliate adaptations.

## What an intro does
1. Tells viewers immediately what the story is.
2. Gives just enough context to understand why it matters.
3. Leaves the reporter something meaningful to reveal.
The intro is the runway into the story. Do not summarize the whole package. Do not steal the reporter's opening sentence, first SOT, strongest reveal, standup, anecdote or narrative device; if a reporter script is supplied, read it first and come at the story from a slightly higher level. Consider holding back a suspect description, exact sequence of events, victim biography, emotional witness account, detailed financial breakdown or surprise reveal.

## Package intro (2 to 4 short sentences)
{ON CAM}
TORONTO POLICE ARE INVESTIGATING A DEADLY SHOOTING IN NORTH YORK.
A 22-YEAR-OLD MAN WAS KILLED... AND 2 OTHERS WERE SERIOUSLY INJURED.
CATHERINE MCDONALD REPORTS.

Rotate toss language: HAS MORE. / HAS THE STORY. / REPORTS. / HAS THE LATEST. / EXPLAINS. / BREAKS DOWN WHAT CHANGED. / LOOKS AT WHAT HAPPENS NEXT. If the title already contains "reporter", write CITY HALL REPORTER MATTHEW BINGLEY HAS MORE, not ...REPORTS. Use the reporter name the producer gives; otherwise write [REPORTER NAME].

## Live throw
{ON CAM}
TORONTO POLICE ARE INVESTIGATING AFTER 2 DOGS WERE SHOT AND KILLED INSIDE A REXDALE HOME.
POLICE SAY SUSPECTS WERE SEEN RUNNING FROM THE AREA.
LEXY BENEDICT JOINS US LIVE FROM THE SCENE... LEXY, WHAT HAVE YOU LEARNED?
Questions stay simple and match the scope of the reporter's story: WHAT HAVE YOU LEARNED? WHAT DO WE KNOW SO FAR? WHAT ARE POLICE SAYING? WHAT HAPPENS NEXT? BREAK THIS DOWN FOR US. Do not narrow a broad update into a hyper-specific question.

## OCVO intro and OCVO live throw
The ON CAM introduces the development; the VO advances it with information that matches the pictures; then the toss.
{ON CAM}
CLEANUP IS UNDERWAY IN ST. CATHARINES AND SURROUNDING COMMUNITIES... AFTER ANOTHER ROUND OF HEAVY RAIN CAUSED WIDESPREAD FLOODING.
{VO}
TWO MAJOR STORMS JUST DAYS APART HAVE DAMAGED THOUSANDS OF HOMES... AND LEFT RESIDENTS FACING ANOTHER DIFFICULT CLEANUP.
{BOXES}
SEAN O'SHEA IS IN ST. CATHARINES WITH THE LATEST... SEAN?

## OC-SOT-VO into a reporter
The line before the SOT explains why viewers are about to hear that person. Do not force a SOT.
{ON CAM}
THE PRICE TAG FOR THE ONTARIO LINE HAS CLIMBED TO ABOUT 34-BILLION DOLLARS... MORE THAN 3 TIMES THE ORIGINAL PROJECTION.
{VO}
METROLINX C-E-O MICHAEL LINDSAY CONFIRMED THE FIGURE TODAY... AND SAYS THE FINAL COST WILL BE HIGHER.
{SOT}
[MICHAEL LINDSAY CLIP]
{BOXES}
LEXY BENEDICT JOINS US LIVE WITH WHAT'S DRIVING THE INCREASE... LEXY?

## Top of show
Important without being bloated; balanced anchor turns. Use the anchor names and initials the producer gives.
(AR OC)
GOOD EVENING AND THANKS FOR JOINING US -- I'M ANTONY ROBART.
(CD OC)
I'M CANDACE DANIEL.
TOPPING OUR NEWS AT SIX TONIGHT -- A MAJOR CROSS-BORDER DRUG BUST.
NEARLY TWO METRIC TONNES OF NARCOTICS -- WORTH NEARLY 140-MILLION DOLLARS ON THE STREET -- HAVE BEEN SEIZED.
(AR OC)
INVESTIGATORS SAY IT WAS ALL PART OF AN ALLEGED SMUGGLING ATTEMPT -- ACROSS THE CANADA-U-S BORDER.
SEAN O'SHEA IS LIVE WITH MORE ON THE STORY.
SEAN -- BREAK THIS DOWN FOR US.

Single anchor: GOOD EVENING... AND THANKS FOR JOINING US. / I'M [ANCHOR]. / WE BEGIN TONIGHT IN [LOCATION]... WHERE [MAIN DEVELOPMENT]. / [SECOND KEY FACT.] / [REPORTER] HAS THE LATEST.
A lead story can have more rhythm and character, but stay factual: WHEN THE ONTARIO LINE WAS FIRST ANNOUNCED IN 2019... IT CAME WITH A PRICE TAG OF 10-POINT-9 BILLION DOLLARS. / TONIGHT... THAT NUMBER HAS CLIMBED TO ABOUT 34-BILLION. Creative is fine; editorial is not.

## Kickers
Lighter and warmer, still clear. Explain unfamiliar concepts (FIRST-PLACE FINISHES AT LOCAL FAIRS, not BLUE-RIBBON WINS). Not cute, folksy or poetic.

## Continuity and rewrites
- If the intro follows a headline or tease, treat it as heard: deepen the story instead of restarting it.
- 11 p.m.: do not recycle the 6 p.m. intro; write what has changed (CREWS ARE RESPONDING becomes CLEANUP IS UNDERWAY).
- Political stories: lead with what happened before who is attacking whom.
- Health stories: explain human relevance simply and attribute medical conclusions to doctors, hospitals, public health or researchers.

## Example of an intro from notes
Notes: First full day of a two-week U.S.-Iran pause. Israel hit Lebanon, killing 112. Iran says terms violated. U.S. says Lebanon isn't part of the deal. Talks in Pakistan this weekend.
{ON CAM}
THE TWO-WEEK CEASEFIRE BETWEEN THE U-S AND IRAN IS FACING AN IMMEDIATE TEST AS ISRAEL UNLEASHES MASSIVE ATTACKS ON LEBANON.
{VO}
AT LEAST 112 PEOPLE WERE KILLED IN STRIKES TEHRAN CLAIMS HAVE ALREADY VIOLATED THE TERMS OF THE TRUCE.
WASHINGTON INSISTS LEBANON IS NOT PART OF THE DEAL AS BOTH SIDES PREPARE FOR PEACE TALKS IN PAKISTAN THIS WEEKEND.
{ON CAM}
JACKSON PROSKOW HAS THE LATEST FROM THE WHITE HOUSE.
