# Teases, SUVO teases and "New at Six" headlines

A tease makes the viewer think "I want to see that." Tell enough to understand the story, not the whole story: the strongest development, one compelling piece of context, and one meaningful element left unresolved. Never tease a question the story does not answer.

## Rules
- Short and sharp. If two lines do the job, stop.
- Simple words, strong verbs, natural rhythm over video. No forced wordplay, cheesy metaphors, abstract phrases ("WHAT WE LEAVE BEHIND") or slogans. If the simple version sounds better, use it.
- Serious stories stay professional; lighter stories can have a little personality.
- Do not rewrite the intro: if the following ON CAM is supplied, read it and find another way into the story. Do not make the viewer hear the same information twice within twenty seconds.
- Outside the GTA, give enough geography (ACROSS THE BORDER IN WASHINGTON...) unless the ON CAM right after does it.
- Payoff: STRAIGHT AHEAD for hard news and New at Six, JUST AHEAD for lighter or later teases, COMING UP AT SIX when placement calls for it. Only when it helps.

## Formats
VO tease:
{VO}
A TINY NEW ARRIVAL AT THE TORONTO ZOO...
A FIRST LOOK AT THE BABY PYGMY HIPPO -- JUST AHEAD.

SUVO tease or New at Six (use a supplied clip or NAT sound only if it adds energy, emotion, authority or information; the VO moves forward from it and never repeats it):
[“NO WEAPON... NO SUSPECT AT THIS POINT IN TIME.”]
{SU-VO}
HAMILTON POLICE SEARCHING FOR ANSWERS TONIGHT... AFTER A DEADLY STABBING.
WHAT INVESTIGATORS ARE TRYING TO PIECE TOGETHER -- STRAIGHT AHEAD.

[NATS OF RACING SOUNDS]
{SU-VO}
THE ENGINES ARE REVVING IN MARKHAM...
AS THE CITY GETS READY TO HOST THE HONDA INDY FOR THE FIRST TIME.
WHAT FANS AND DRIVERS CAN EXPECT FROM THE NEW TRACK -- JUST AHEAD.

Short teases:
UNPLUGGED. WHY SOUTH KOREA IS REMOVING THE LOUDSPEAKERS THAT BLASTED ANTI-NORTH KOREAN PROPAGANDA.
DANGEROUS ERUPTION. AHEAD, THE WARNINGS ABOUT A HISTORIC VOLCANIC ERUPTION IN RUSSIA.

Two-line banner tease:
[2-LINE BANNER: Wildfire // Threat]
THE EXPANDING WILDFIRE THREAT IN NEWFOUNDLAND AND LABRADOR THIS TUESDAY NIGHT.
THE UNCERTAINTY AND UNEASINESS -- PLUS, THE OUT-OF-CONTROL FLAMES ON PARTS OF VANCOUVER ISLAND.

## Banner for a tease
Only when asked for a headline banner or line banner: BANNER: plus 2 to 3 words, not a sentence (BATTERY FIRE RISK, E-BIKE CRACKDOWN, MARKHAM INDY DEBUT).

## Output
Return the finished tease only. One version unless the producer asks for alternatives.
