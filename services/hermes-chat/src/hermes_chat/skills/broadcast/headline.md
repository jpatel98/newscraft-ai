# TV news headline (ANT VO)

A short line over video that tells viewers what is coming while keeping enough curiosity to keep them watching. Start with the strongest visual, emotional or factual hook. Build toward the package, live hit or next segment. Use urgency only when the facts support it. Production notes in square brackets only when needed. Add a one-line or two-line banner only when asked.

## Format
{ANT VO}
TORONTO'S MAYOR DETAILING HER MUCH-ANTICIPATED ELECTION-YEAR BUDGET TONIGHT.
HOW OLIVIA CHOW PLANS TO HELP RESIDENTS WITH AFFORDABILITY -- JUST AHEAD.

## Examples
{ANT VO}
IT'S NOT AVAILABLE TO THE PUBLIC YET... BUT IT'S GOT A PLAQUE.
POLITICAL LEADERS GATHER TODAY TO CELEBRATE THIS WEEKEND'S OPENING OF THE EGLINTON CROSSTOWN.

{ANT VO}
IT'S YEARS OVERDUE... AND WAY OVER BUDGET.
BUT FINALLY, THE EGLINTON L-R-T IS ABOUT TO OPEN.
WHEN YOU CAN CLIMB ABOARD -- STRAIGHT AHEAD.

{ANT VO}
THE SEX ASSAULT TRIAL OF BILLIONAIRE BUSINESSMAN FRANK STRONACH HAS BEEN DELAYED.
WE'RE AT THE COURTHOUSE WITH THE EXPLANATION.

{ANT VO}

WHAT THE CROWN AND DEFENCE ARE CLAIMING AS CLOSING ARGUMENTS ARE HEARD -- STRAIGHT AHEAD.
