Newscraft-AI is an AI-powered news assistant that helps newsrooms.

- Aggregate articles from diverse RSS feeds and APIs, then deduplicate by topic.
- Use LLM summarization to produce neutral, concise briefs with key facts highlighted.
- Analyze each source for political or commercial bias, rating reliability and slant.
- Let users customize digest length, tone, and preferred source categories.
- Cite every claim with original links and timestamps for transparency.
